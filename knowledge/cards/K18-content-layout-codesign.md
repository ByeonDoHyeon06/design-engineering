# K18 — Content and layout should be designed together

**Knowledge type:** PRACTITIONER + DESIGN HYPOTHESIS  
**Source IDs:** N40, N41  
**Verification level:** Official GOV.UK practitioner guidance, not experimental evidence for a specific component arrangement.  
**Verified claim IDs:** none

## When to retrieve
A generic card grid forces unequal content into equal boxes; translated strings overflow; navigation labels fail to reflect task intent; content arrives after wireframes are frozen.

## Source-grounded scope
- **N40:** Content design should start with user needs and consider quantity, format, and where information belongs.
- **N41:** GOV.UK guidance identifies needs in terms of who, what action and why; this does not mandate a web page template.

## Design operations (hypotheses to test)
1. Inventory content at realistic lengths. Protect verified facts, data relationships, actions, disclaimers and mandatory labels.
2. Assign content roles: orientation, evidence, comparison, sequence, primary action, secondary detail, warning, feedback.
3. Map roles to reading and task order; ask whether a timeline, comparison table, narrative sequence, grouping or no visual component best serves them.
4. Explore at least two **structurally different** arrangements if there is meaningful uncertainty. Avoid diversity for its own sake.
5. Prototype using real Korean and mixed-language content before making spacing and containers final.
6. Verify scan order, heading context, contrast, keyboard order, long-text and empty/loading/error variants.
7. Revise *both* wording and layout based on rendered observations; do not truncate important qualifications merely to preserve symmetry.

## Countercases
- Real data and product constraints outrank rhetorical elegance.
- A repeated card system may be best for genuinely comparable items; variation is not an intrinsic goal.
- A source that teaches content design does not experimentally validate this exact procedure.

## Validation
Test a representative user finding an item, comparing choices and recovering from an error. Inspect rendered 1440/390/320 widths before/after revision, noting task results and unresolved tradeoffs.
