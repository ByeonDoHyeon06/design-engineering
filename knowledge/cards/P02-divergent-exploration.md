# P02 — Explore meaningful alternatives before converging

**Type:** RESEARCH  — **Source IDs:** CR3, DC2, CR4

## Use when
A substantial design brief admits distinct plausible interpretations or compositions.

## Evidence and scope
Parallel prototyping was beneficial in a particular experimental design task; the magnitude and transfer to AI agents remain unknown. Refer to `knowledge/sources.json` for original source URLs and access limits.

## Transferable reasoning moves
1. Articulate two contrasting hypotheses about what should carry attention or how a task should be organized.
2. Make cheap representations sufficient to expose differences—structure, content map, type hierarchy or interaction sketch.
3. Critique each against the same constraints; do not inflate content to make them look different.
4. Select one idea and improve execution instead of distributing effort across many polished variants.

## Do not infer
- The parallel-prototype experiment used novice designers and graphical ads, not an instruction to build exactly 3 sites.
- Changing only palette or illustrations does not count as a different design hypothesis.

## Verify the output
Can a reviewer explain one substantive difference in information/interaction/meaning between alternatives?
