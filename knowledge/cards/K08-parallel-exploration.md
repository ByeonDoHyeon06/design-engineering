# K08 — Explore different problem hypotheses before polishing one

**Knowledge type:** RESEARCH  
**Source IDs:** CR1, CR2, CR3  
**Verification level:** SOURCE-IDENTITY + PUBLISHER/INSTITUTION ABSTRACT (or OFFICIAL NORMATIVE TEXT for WCAG). Full paper review is **not** claimed.  
**Verified claim IDs:** VC10, VC11, VC12

## When to retrieve
The first familiar SaaS layout keeps returning despite different briefs.

## Verified source findings and study scope
- **Evidence — CR1:** Jansson & Smith’s conceptual-design experiments document design fixation under studied examples.
- **Evidence — CR2:** A 43-study meta-analysis finds examples can reduce *variety of categories* while improving *novelty/quality* under examined conditions. Both can be true.
- **Evidence — CR3:** In a controlled novice web-ad design study, parallel prototyping before feedback yielded stronger measured advertisement outcomes and diversity than serial prototyping.
- **Transfer limitation:** Human design experiments do not establish that three AI prompts or specific agent workflows automatically improve results.

## Transferable reasoning moves — *derived design advice, not direct experimental findings*
1. Draft distinct problem interpretations before variations in color/type; avoid three superficial theme swaps.
2. Contrast each idea on meaning, constraints, likely failure mode and testable hypothesis.
3. Select one to refine with actual feedback; do not maximize divergence for its own sake or impose a quota for every small task.

## Countercases and overclaim guard
- Explicitly review source-specific constraints in `knowledge/verified/claims.json` and source access levels in `knowledge/sources.json`.
- Do not turn a studied observation into a mandatory style rule, numerical CSS constant, or general guarantee of user performance.
- The agent must not assert that this study validated AI-generated UI, unless such testing exists elsewhere.

## Verification in a live design
Judge both diversity of *underlying concepts* and final artifact quality; test end results rather than number of sketches.
