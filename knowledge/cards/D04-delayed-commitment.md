# D04 — Keep exploratory uncertainty alive just long enough

**Type:** PRACTITIONER  — **Documented perspective:** bierut  — **Source ID:** PM4

## Useful problem signal
Resist prematurely declaring the first coherent design the best design.

## Portable design move
1. Capture initial assumptions.
2. Try a non-obvious reorganization or alternative story.
3. Converge once evidence or constraints discriminate options.

## Provenance boundary
Do not fetishize notebooks or endlessly brainstorm; oral history is a personal account.
