# D02 — Make the artifact understandable, useful and honest

**Type:** PRACTITIONER  — **Documented perspective:** rams  — **Source ID:** PM2

## Useful problem signal
Challenge every feature and visual cue to explain what it enables or clarifies.

## Portable design move
1. Identify friction or misleading affordances.
2. Remove or revise features without a user-relevant purpose.
3. Inspect final details and failure/recovery states.

## Provenance boundary
Not an instruction to make every design minimal or mono-chrome; museum summaries are practitioner evidence.

Check `methods/designer-methods.json` and `knowledge/sources.json` for institutional/primary URLs and the actual access level. This is a perspective to question, **not** a persona to emulate or an aesthetic to reproduce.
