# P16 — Build coherent identity from meaning, not visual repetition

**Type:** RESEARCH  — **Source IDs:** A10, A11, A15, PM1

## Use when
A visual system needs memorability but cannot depend only on a signature illustration or color.

## Evidence and scope
Brand research suggests multiple design properties contribute to impressions, not a universal visual code. Refer to `knowledge/sources.json` for original source URLs and access limits.

## Transferable reasoning moves
1. Describe what the brand or product intends to communicate.
2. Translate that purpose into typography, hierarchy, graphic relations, states and material expression.
3. Use repetition for semantic consistency; vary expressions where content calls for it.
4. Inspect the system across real sections and small mobile sizes.

## Do not infer
- Logo- and typeface-impression findings do not prove whole-website conversions.
- Do not clone identifiable practitioner signatures or brand trade dress.

## Verify the output
Test comprehension, intended impression or recognition with actual target audiences where possible.
