# D03 — Interrogate assumptions before committing to form

**Type:** PRACTITIONER  — **Documented perspective:** scher  — **Source ID:** PM3

## Useful problem signal
Ask what the stated brief might be missing and what matters to users or the organization.

## Portable design move
1. List assumptions and unknowns without rewriting given facts.
2. Select the most consequential question.
3. Use its answer to propose a testable visual or content hypothesis.

## Provenance boundary
An interview documents a practitioner’s way of approaching some clients; not a rule to copy iconic Scher typography.

Check `methods/designer-methods.json` and `knowledge/sources.json` for institutional/primary URLs and the actual access level. This is a perspective to question, **not** a persona to emulate or an aesthetic to reproduce.
