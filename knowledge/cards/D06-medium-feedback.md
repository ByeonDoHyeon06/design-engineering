# D06 — Prototype within the medium and shorten feedback loops

**Type:** PRACTITIONER  — **Documented perspective:** cooper  — **Source ID:** PM6

## Useful problem signal
A static comp may hide what happens in actual interaction or responsive environments.

## Portable design move
1. Express the hypothesis in the target medium.
2. Inspect real temporal/interactive behavior.
3. Revise typography, flow and feedback with the observed result.

## Provenance boundary
Not a mandate for kinetic or 3D typography. An exhibition text is interpretive institutional evidence.

Check `methods/designer-methods.json` and `knowledge/sources.json` for institutional/primary URLs and the actual access level. This is a perspective to question, **not** a persona to emulate or an aesthetic to reproduce.
