# D01 — Clarify meaning, internal language and pragmatic use

**Type:** PRACTITIONER  — **Documented perspective:** vignelli  — **Source ID:** PM1

## Useful problem signal
Ask three different questions: what does it mean, what makes the language coherent, and does it work for its audience?

## Portable design move
1. Write the intended message without styling.
2. Inspect whether repeated elements express the same underlying relationship.
3. Prototype and ask users to articulate what they understood.
