# P03 — Detect and escape design fixation

**Type:** RESEARCH  — **Source IDs:** CR1, CR2

## Use when
First idea, existing examples or an initial style selection are narrowing exploration.

## Evidence and scope
Original studies concern human ideation; applying an anti-fixation check to a model is an engineering hypothesis. Refer to `knowledge/sources.json` for original source URLs and access limits.

## Transferable reasoning moves
1. Identify the first example or assumption anchoring the design.
2. List features copied from the example and the *reason* each might matter.
3. Deliberately consider an alternative causal mechanism (different hierarchy, grouping, expression or interaction).
4. Select on fit and verified quality, not novelty for its own sake.

## Do not infer
- Examples can also improve novelty and solution quality; no blanket ban on references.
- Do not replace fixation with random stylistic divergence.

## Verify the output
Review the artifact for inherited shapes with no functional or semantic rationale.
