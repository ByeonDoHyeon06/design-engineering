# K17 — Korean UX writing: meaning before fluency

**Knowledge type:** PRACTITIONER + DESIGN HYPOTHESIS  
**Source IDs:** N40  
**Verification level:** Official GOV.UK practitioner guidance about **English** content design; Korean adaptation is a contextual hypothesis, NOT a studied transfer.  
**Verified claim IDs:** none (no Korean-language empirical study verified here)

## When to retrieve
UI text is grammatically plausible but sounds translated, vague or marketing-heavy; action labels do not say what happens; form errors give no recovery advice.

## Source-grounded scope
- **N40:** GOV.UK content-design guidance begins with user needs, content format and plain English. This is an English public-sector practice guide, not a rule of Korean grammar or proof of user outcomes in Korean products.

## Design operations (context-dependent judgment)
1. Identify who speaks, who acts, audience, intent, voice level and mandatory brand/legal terminology before rewriting.
2. Preserve factual claims, precise constraints, meaning, conditions, timing, quantities and the user's intended action.
3. Prefer natural Korean syntax; flag potential translationese, abstract nouns, redundant subjects, vague benefit claims, needless passives and mixed politeness **for human judgment**.
4. Write actionable labels around user intent (e.g. '일정 확인' rather than generic '자세히'). Ensure the label matches the actual next state.
5. For errors: explain what happened, where possible why, and a concrete next step without blame. For empty/success states: specify what is empty or completed.
6. Test copy **in the UI**, not just in a document; check wrap, truncation, a11y labels, semantic action order and tone consistency.
7. Read all final Korean strings aloud when practical, then solicit a native-speaker review for high-impact or public-facing copy.

## Review examples (illustrative, not universal replacements)
- Vague: '혁신적인 솔루션을 경험해 보세요' → Concrete **only if true**: '프로젝트별 접근 권한을 관리합니다.'
- Unhelpful: '오류가 발생했습니다' → Actionable **when diagnosis known**: '파일을 올리지 못했습니다. 연결 상태를 확인한 뒤 다시 시도해 주세요.'
- Generic: '확인' → task-specific: '예약 내용 확인' **when that is the actual action**.

## Countercases
- Legal, medical and product terminology may need exact wording. Never simplify away conditions or invent features.
- Not all passive constructions are errors. Brand copy can be expressive when clarity is preserved.
- This card cannot automatically prove grammatical correctness or native-like naturalness.

## Validation
Review Korean headings, buttons, empty/error/success states and form labels as real tasks. Record original → risk → revised → preserved meaning → screenshot/task proof. No unsupported claims of measured comprehension.
