# P07 — Balance rhythm, proportion, unity and tension intentionally

**Type:** RESEARCH  — **Source IDs:** A3, A4, A5, A6, A9, A13

## Use when
A screen is functional but unconvincing, flat, misweighted or visually overworked.

## Evidence and scope
Composition studies include models and abstract stimuli; transfer to interfaces is a hypothesis to be judged in context. Refer to `knowledge/sources.json` for original source URLs and access limits.

## Transferable reasoning moves
1. Locate focal point, supporting hierarchy and quiet regions using actual content.
2. Sketch at least one alternative that redistributes emphasis rather than adding ornament.
3. Review repetition and variation across a screen/flow; adjust rhythm to meaning.
4. Inspect cropped mobile variants; remove forms that distract from priority tasks.

## Do not infer
- No golden ratio, symmetry maximization, universal visual center-of-mass score or mandatory grids.
- Visual-tension studies based on basic shapes do not validate rotated form controls.

## Verify the output
Review screenshots at target sizes and compare readability, focal sequence, visual weight and craft.
