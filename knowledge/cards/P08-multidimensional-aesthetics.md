# P08 — Evaluate perceived aesthetics as multiple dimensions

**Type:** RESEARCH  — **Source IDs:** A1, A2, A12, E5, R7

## Use when
A design seems polished but ordinary, or striking but difficult to understand.

## Evidence and scope
Classical/expressive aesthetics and VisAWI dimensions describe distinct facets, not a universal target style. Refer to `knowledge/sources.json` for original source URLs and access limits.

## Transferable reasoning moves
1. Evaluate clarity/order and creative expressiveness independently.
2. Inspect simplicity, diversity, color relationship and craftsmanship rather than an overall “pretty” verdict.
3. Choose appropriate methods for perceived visual quality; do not translate that score into task efficiency.
4. Identify both visual strengths and costs of the chosen expression.

## Do not infer
- Do not claim a custom 12/100-point score is a validated measurement scale.
- R7 did not show objective performance benefit in its tested conditions; E5 concerns perceived usability.

## Verify the output
If user perception matters, use correctly administered instruments or multiple blinded reviewers; measure behavior separately.
