# D07 — Move between user learning, divergent possibilities and prototypes

**Type:** PRACTITIONER  — **Documented perspective:** ideo  — **Source ID:** PM7

## Useful problem signal
Treat a design process as flexible inquiry rather than a fixed sequence of workshops.

## Portable design move
1. Identify what the user is trying to do.
2. Generate competing hypotheses.
3. Test the cheapest meaningful representation and update the hypothesis.

IDEO describes collective practice, not a universal scientifically validated five-stage formula.
