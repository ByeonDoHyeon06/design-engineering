# P05 — Express information relationships through grouping

**Type:** RESEARCH  — **Source IDs:** E3, A3

## Use when
Elements appear related but the meaning is unclear, or unrelated content appears as a single group.

## Evidence and scope
Gestalt-style grouping helps explain perception but should be checked in the UI context. Refer to `knowledge/sources.json` for original source URLs and access limits.

## Transferable reasoning moves
1. Describe semantic relationships before drawing their borders.
2. Use proximity, shared region, alignment and repetition to show real relationships.
3. Avoid decorative containers that imply false grouping.
4. Inspect scanning and comprehension at the final viewport.

## Do not infer
- Palmer studied common-region perceptual grouping, not universal cards or 8px spacing.
- Abstract aesthetic metrics are diagnostic vocabulary, not a formula to optimize.

## Verify the output
Ask a reviewer to group content by meaning without looking at the design source tree.
